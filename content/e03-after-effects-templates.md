## How to pick and use project files

| Goal | Pick |
|---|---|
| Reuse in client work | Framer SaaS UI, Base 44 SaaS Promo, aepmotion Pro |
| Learn how pro SaaS edits are built | EarnEdits, Motion with Nas, Motionmaxed, Emma VSL |
| Most projects per dollar | Madbax, Mhd Labs store |
| Build your own SaaS videos fast | SaaSor, Solair SaaS Kit, Dream Chaser kit |
| DaVinci Resolve instead of AE | Prinsedits |

**Before you buy**
1. **Check the AE version.** Motion Buoy needs AE 2026; Mhd Labs needs 2025+; Framer works from 2024.
2. **Check fonts and plugins.** NAS.io and Mhd Labs include fonts; EarnEdits doesn't.
3. **Check the licence** against the table above.

**After you open one**
- **File → Dependencies → Collect Files** to keep a clean, self-contained copy.
- Swap AE 27's new **3D materials** in for flat fills to make it your own ([E02](e02-after-effects-27-updates.md)).
- Let [Atom](e01-atom-guide.md) do the rebrand: *"Change every colour to our brand palette and expose them on one control layer."*
